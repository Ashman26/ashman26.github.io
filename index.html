How I Approach Data Problems

A systematic methodology for turning requirements into effective data solutions.

1

Understand

Identify the business question and requirements. What decision needs to be made? What information is needed?

2

Explore

Understand the data sources, structures, and quality. Map the data landscape and identify dependencies.

3

Transform

Prepare, integrate, and validate the data. Apply business logic and ensure accuracy and consistency.

4

Deliver

Build reports, dashboards, or automated workflows. Present information in a clear, actionable format.

5

Improve

Review accuracy, usability, and maintainability. Look for opportunities to enhance performance and insights.
